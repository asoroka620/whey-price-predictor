Total stocks: The sum of all measured opening or ending inventory variables within the 
domestic supply chain at a specific point in time, required to balance total supply 
with total disposition
    Essentially, the entire total including imports

Manufactures and government stocks: 
    Manufacture: All stocks held by manufacturers, excluding stock held on Canadian 
    Dairy Commission Accounts

    Government: All stocks held by the Canadian Dairy Commission, 
    including stocks held on behalf of the federal government and provincial governments
